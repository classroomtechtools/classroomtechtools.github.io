Sometimes, not knowing is part of the joy

7 August 2026 Yuri Kim 한국어로 제공됩니다

A month ago, I booked Selena K.’s “#Afterhours” private dining and mentioned that it would fall on our wedding anniversary.

Then, on the day, Adam Morris and I completely forgot. 😂

During dinner, Selena said that we do not create enough opportunities to celebrate with each other. She then invited everyone to celebrate our anniversary with us.

To be honest, it was a big surprise as we had not expected it at all.

In fact, the whole evening was filled with surprises.

We did not know what cuisine or food would be served, or who the other guests would be.

We were welcomed with a message on the table, gifts from #RhymbaHills and #ClarieOrganics. A table was beautifully decorated at #ColorKing and a wonderful food and bubble pairing made the night special.

And the food itself was on another level.

In life, we often try to plan ahead and keep things under control.

Yet that evening, because we knew so little about what to expect, we were able to enjoy the moment and create space for surprise, connection.

Uncertainty may not always bring anxiety and tension.

Knowing everything in advance can take away some of the fun. So when we do not know what to expect, we can experience an even greater surprise, excitement, curiosity and joy.

Thank Selena and Daniel Goh for creating such a memorable evening.

Thank everyone for being such great company and celebrating our 22th anniversary together.

❓ When was the last time you enjoyed something without knowing exactly how it would unfold?
