Let me reintroduce myself: Celebrating 5K Followers

16 February 2024 Yuri Kim 한국어로 제공됩니다

It’s been over a year since I actively engaged on LinkedIn and I’m happy to share that I’ve exceeded 5K followers. I’m grateful to all those who have supported me throughout this journey. I’d like to take this opportunity to reintroduce myself formally to those who may not be familiar with my professional journey.

  1. International Educator, ICF Accredited Coach, Positive Psychology Specialist and Educator👩🏻💼

I am a passionate international educator and a professional life coach accredited by ICF, specialising in #positivepsychology and positive education. With 22 years of experience teaching in #internationalschools, I offer coaching and consulting services for teens, parents and adults. Based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, I collaborates with schools and educational organisations, conducting workshops and educational programmes.

  1. Lang & Lit Teacher, Head of Department, Grade Level Coordinator, Cambridge & IB Examiner👩🏻🏫

With an extensive educational background and diverse teaching experience in countries such as Korea, China, Australia, Vietnam, and Malaysia, I have taught a variety of subjects, including ESL/EAL, Chinese, Korean LL, and music for secondary students. I have also served as a Cambridge and IB examiner, enhancing my educational expertise. Additionally, I have taken on leadership roles as Head of Department and Grade Level Coordinator to support fellow teachers, parents, and students.

  1. An educator, coach, and mentor leading change through a positive growth mindset🤗

I foster an environment where individual tendencies, beliefs, values, and diversity are respected. I serve as a bridging role to explore new opportunities through mutual trust, respect and cooperation. I seek to provide a positive growth mindset and emotional support, helping individuals focus on current goals, foster strength and find direction to envision their future dreams!
