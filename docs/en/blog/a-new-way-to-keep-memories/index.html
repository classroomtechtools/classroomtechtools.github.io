A New Way to Keep Memories

3 September 2026 Yuri Kim 한국어로 제공됩니다

I am not good at putting my experiences into words or writing a diary regularly. I wish I had started the habit earlier and I am trying to make a habit of it.

A few days ago, I used AI to create a visual diary of the last Sunday in August when I enjoyed fondue and wine with friends.

It started with a visit to my regular wine shop. The owner, who is also a friend, offered me a glass of wine and  special cheese she had ordered from France.

I enjoyed the cheese so much that I mentioned how good it would be for fondue. And I told her that I had a fondue set.

So when we talked about it, we just picked a Sunday afternoon to get together with other friends.

We spent a lovely and relaxed afternoon enjoying each other’s company. I went home feeling happy and very well fed.

While putting this visual diary together, I realised how much I enjoy being around people who are passionate about what they love, are authentic and generously share what they have.

Sharing our different experiences, thoughts and laughter over good food and wine is one of my favourite things to do.

I only added a few thoughts to a photo and now I have a little story I want to share.

Perhaps I am discovering a new way of keeping memories that I can enjoy.

❓How do you capture the moments you want to remember?
