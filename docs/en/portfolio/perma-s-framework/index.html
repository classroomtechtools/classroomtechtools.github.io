Workshop for Educators

PERMA-S Framework

💫 Who are the like-minded people who bring out the best in you?

I’m still feeling inspired after attending the EdInspire event in Dulwich College (Singapore) last weekend.

I was surrounded by like-minded educators, coaches, changemakers who share a passion for hashtag#growth, hashtag#connection and meaningful hashtag#impact.

A heartfelt thank you to Sarah Aldous, Flora Xu, and EdEvents Community David Burke for creating such a powerful space for growth and reflection. I can imagine the immense effort behind organising an event of this scale and impact.

It was an honour to facilitate a workshop exploring the PERMA-S framework in positive psychology and how to use strengths-based feedback to support our communities.

I also attended sessions on personal growth and coaching. Each workshop reaffirmed why I chose this path, reminding me of the power of connection and lifelong learning. To Dan Brown, Samantha Chesler, Patricia Hubert and Liam Benjamin thank you for your powerful sessions.

Deep appreciation to the keynote speakers, Dan Haesler, Ceci Gomez-Galvez and Diana Osagie💫 for sharing your expertise and experiences. Your words made me pause and reflect on who I am, what I value, and how I can live and work with intention.

It was also wonderful to reconnect with familiar faces, catching up with Bora Rancic, Shruti Sundararaman, Florencio Ricohermoso (since we first met in Korea September) and with Kimiko Fujii after 12 years since our SSIS days. These reunions made the event even more special.

Finally, Thank everyone who joined my workshop for your openness, presence, and insights. I learned just as much from each of you. Chris LeBlanc, Sarah Powell, thank you for sharing your perspectives and I really enjoyed our interactions.

Events like this remind me of what inspires us to rise, to grow, and to show up as our best selves. Let’s continue to support one another with intention, courage, and an open heart.
